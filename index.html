I’m an Electrical & Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the Islamic University of Technology, Bangladesh.

My work brings together EV simulation, digital electronics, robotics, and computational modeling. I enjoy the process of taking a question apart, building a model, and finding out what it can teach me.

I’m particularly interested in photonics and interdisciplinary engineering research. Through faculty-supervised work and research-style competitions, I’m building a foundation for hands-on research.
